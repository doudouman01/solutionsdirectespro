## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do ADHD planners actually work?

Yes — when they are designed for ADHD brains specifically. Generic planners fail because they assume executive function that ADHD brains lack. ADHD-specific tools work around this by using visual cues, micro-steps, dopamine triggers, and flexible structures instead of rigid schedules.

### What is the most important ADHD tool to start with?

The Brain Dump Planner or Task Paralysis Breaker. Both address the most immediate ADHD pain points — mental overload and inability to start — and provide relief within the first use.

### Can digital tools replace paper planners for ADHD?

For some people, yes. But many ADHD experts recommend paper tools because the physical act of writing engages different neural pathways than typing. The tactile experience also reduces the temptation to switch to another app — a constant risk with digital tools.
