## She was hired to replace a dead woman. But the dead woman isn't dead.

When Sienna Cole answers an unusual job posting — live-in nanny for two children in a remote Maine mansion, $15,000 a month — she convinces herself it's about the money. But no. It's about Eleanor.

**Eleanor Ashford**, the wife of wealthy financier Marcus Ashford, died six months ago in a car crash on a fog-shrouded coastal road. At least, that's the official version.

Sienna doesn't believe it. Because Eleanor was her half-sister — a sister she didn't know existed until she discovered her dying mother's letters. And now, Sienna lives in Eleanor's house, sleeps in her bed, wears her clothes, and raises her children.

## Something is wrong in the Ashford house.

Those children who jump at the slightest noise. That housekeeper who observes everything. This husband whose charm is never truly reflected in his eyes.

And that locked door on the third floor that no one wants to explain.

## What Readers Will Find Inside

- A gothic psychological thriller set in a remote Maine coastal mansion
- A heroine who steps into a dead woman's life — and discovers the woman may not be dead
- Themes of identity, obsession, and the terrifying cost of replacing someone
- A husband whose perfection is a performance and a house full of locked secrets
- The first volume of a trilogy

## The Question at the Heart of the Novel

If you take the place of a deceased woman, do you also inherit her fate?

*The Replacement Wife* is a gothic psychological thriller that explores identity, obsession, and the line between uncovering the truth and becoming its next victim.
