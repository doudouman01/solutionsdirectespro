Every guest at your table deserves an exceptional drink.

Christmas is a time of intention — the music, the table, the food, every detail chosen with care. Why should what's in the glass be any different? **The Christmas Cocktail Bible** is the definitive guide to holiday drink-making.

Inside, you'll find over 50 original recipes organized into three collections: warm cocktails that feel like a fireplace in a glass, cold creations that carry the depth and elegance of winter, and premium mocktails so refined your guests will reach for them by choice.

## What's Inside

**Warm Cocktails** — Imperial Mulled Wine, Spiced Dark Chocolate, Christmas Grog Reimagined, Elegant Christmas Coffee, and more.

**Cold Cocktails** — Christmas Ruby, Snowdrift, Frosted Pine, Polar Night, Elegant Celebration, and more.

**Premium Mocktails** — Sparkling Spice Infusion, Spiced Cold Chocolate, Elegant Cranberry, No-Compromise Celebration, and more.

## What Makes This Book Different

- **Batch versions** scaled for 12 and 25 guests — no guesswork, no scrambling
- **Food pairings** for every drink — the right appetizer, dessert, or meal companion
- **Difficulty and cost ratings** at a glance — know exactly what each recipe demands
- **Both US and metric measurements** in every recipe
- **A complete hosting guide** — the right glass, the right ice, the right garnish

## The Complete Host

Ready-made drink menus, glassware guide, flavored ice techniques, atmosphere tips, and a perfect evening checklist. Whether you're hosting an intimate dinner for four or a grand holiday party for fifty, this book gives you everything you need — from the first garnish to the last toast.

*No professional bartending experience required. No professional equipment needed. Just intention, good ingredients, and this book.*
