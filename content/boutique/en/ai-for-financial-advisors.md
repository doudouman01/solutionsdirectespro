The most comprehensive AI guide for financial advisors ever published — 745 pages, 66 ready-to-use prompts, 5 complete workflows, and 5 real-world case studies.

While other books give you a handful of prompts and call it a day, **AI for Financial Advisors** is the definitive resource that transforms how you run your entire practice.

Whether you're a solo RIA, a team leader at a wirehouse, or a growing independent advisor, this book gives you everything you need to integrate AI into every aspect of your business — from client acquisition to compliance, from portfolio communication to retirement planning.

## What's Inside

- **66 copy-paste ChatGPT prompts** designed specifically for financial advisors — not generic business prompts, but prompts built around SEC/FINRA compliance, fiduciary language, and real advisor workflows
- **5 end-to-end workflows** covering client onboarding, annual reviews, prospect nurturing, compliance documentation, and practice growth
- **5 detailed case studies** showing exactly how advisors at different practice sizes implemented AI and the results they achieved
- **The CREA Method** — a proprietary framework (Contextualize, Refine, Execute, Assess) that turns any advisor task into an AI-optimized process
- **SEC/FINRA compliance guide** for AI-generated content, record-keeping requirements, and supervisory obligations
- **A 7-Day Action Plan** to go from AI-curious to AI-powered in one week
- **20 FAQ** answering the most common concerns advisors have about AI adoption
- **Complete glossary** of AI and fintech terminology for advisors

**Part I: AI Foundations for Advisors** — Understanding AI in the context of financial services.

**Part III: Financial Planning & Analysis** — AI-assisted portfolio analysis, retirement projections, and scenario modeling.

**Part IV: Marketing & Business Development** — AI-driven prospecting, content creation, and practice growth.

**Part V: Compliance & Risk Management** — Navigating SEC/FINRA requirements with AI, documentation, and audit readiness.

**Part VI: Practice Operations & Efficiency** — Streamlining workflows, automating administrative tasks, and scaling your practice.

**Part VII: Advanced AI Strategies** — Cutting-edge applications and future-proofing your practice.

**Part VIII: Implementation & Action** — The 7-Day Action Plan and ongoing optimization.
