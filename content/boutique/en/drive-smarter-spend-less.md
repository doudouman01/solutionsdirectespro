**Drive Smarter, Spend Less** is the most comprehensive fuel-saving guide ever written for everyday American drivers. Backed by real data, field-tested techniques, and zero gimmicks, this book walks you through everything from the way you press the gas pedal to the way you plan your weekly errands.

- 🚗 **21 chapters** covering driving habits, vehicle maintenance, route planning, seasonal tips, and more
- 💰 **Real dollar savings** calculated for every technique — know exactly what each change is worth
- 🔋 **EV & hybrid coverage** — future-proof your savings no matter what you drive
- 🧠 **The psychology of fuel spending** — why you overspend and how to stop
- 📋 **Checklists, action steps & a first-year savings plan** — not just theory, but a system
- 🏢 **Small business fleet strategies** — save thousands across multiple vehicles
- ❌ **20 fuel myths busted** — stop wasting money on tricks that don't work
