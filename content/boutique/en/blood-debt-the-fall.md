A gritty crime thriller set in the streets of Baltimore, where Detective Nathan Cole hunts a killer whose murders are payments in a ledger no one was meant to see.

The victims have nothing in common - except the numbers carved into their flesh. Each body is a transaction. Each death settles a score. And the deeper Cole digs, the closer the debt comes to him.

- A relentless Baltimore detective thriller
- Dark, atmospheric, and impossible to put down
- Book 1 in the Blood Debt series

**Everyone owes something. The question is who's collecting.**
