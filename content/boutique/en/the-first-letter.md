Clara thought she knew everything about her grandmother. The quiet life in York. The tea shop on the cobbled street. The gentle smile that never quite reached her eyes.

But the letters tell a different story. A story of forbidden love, impossible choices, and a secret that was buried for sixty years.

As Clara follows the trail from York to Paris to a village in the south of France, she discovers that her grandmother's past holds the key to her own future.

- A beautifully written women's fiction novel spanning decades
- A mystery woven through love letters and hidden truths
- York, Paris, and Provence as unforgettable backdrops

**Some letters are forgotten on purpose. These were hidden to protect a truth too dangerous to speak.**
