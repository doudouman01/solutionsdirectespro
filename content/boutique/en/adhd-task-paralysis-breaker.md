You know what you need to do. You just can't start. The *ADHD Task Paralysis Breaker* is a 15-page printable toolkit designed for the moments when your brain locks up and willpower isn't an option.

Six core tools give you different angles of attack depending on what's keeping you stuck. The 2-Minute Rule shrinks any task to its tiniest starting point. The Body-Brain-Space Matrix helps you figure out what's actually blocking you. The Smallest Possible Step strips away every excuse. The Fear Buster names the real reason you're frozen. Eight pre-written Permission Slips tell you what you need to hear. And the Decision Eliminator removes the "but which task first" problem entirely.

When everything fails, the Emergency Shutdown Protocol gives you a 5-step restart sequence to bring your brain back online. Four Daily Unfreeze Logs help you track what works, and the Freeze Pattern Tracker reveals the triggers you didn't know you had.

Print it, pin it to your wall, load it on your tablet — this is the toolkit you reach for when your brain says "no."
