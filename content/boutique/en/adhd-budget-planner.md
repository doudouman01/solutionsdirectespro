Finally, a budget system that works with your brain, not against it. The *ADHD Budget Planner* is a 35-page printable toolkit designed specifically for neurodivergent minds — no shame, no impossible precision, just simple systems that actually stick.

Built around the 4-Bucket System (Bills, Needs, Wants, Future), this planner replaces overwhelming spreadsheets with clear, visual tools that reduce decision fatigue. Impulse Purchase Pause Pads help you break the buy-now cycle, while the Debt Snowball Tracker and Savings Sprint Tracker turn progress into something you can see and celebrate. Mandatory fun money is built right in — because deprivation budgets never work.

Inside you'll find six monthly budget worksheets, eight weekly money check-ins, quarterly reviews, a subscription audit worksheet, a Money Wins Journal and an ADHD Money Rules Cheat Sheet — everything you need to build a sustainable relationship with your finances.

Print it out, load it on your tablet, or use both — the clean PDF format works beautifully however you prefer to plan.
