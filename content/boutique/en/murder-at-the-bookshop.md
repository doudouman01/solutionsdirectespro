When bookshop owner Nora finds a body tucked behind the biography section on a quiet Tuesday morning, her peaceful life in Birchwood Bay is turned upside down.

The victim was a stranger. But everyone in town seems to know something about him. The baker. The mayor. Even the retired librarian who never misses a thing.

Armed with curiosity, a stubborn streak, and far too much coffee, Nora sets out to find the truth before the killer finds her.

- A cozy mystery set in a charming Canadian lakeside town
- A witty, relatable heroine who cannot leave a mystery alone
- Quirky suspects, red herrings, and a twist you won't see coming

**In Birchwood Bay, the books aren't the only things with killer endings.**
