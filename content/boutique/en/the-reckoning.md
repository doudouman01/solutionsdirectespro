This is not a self-help book that tells you to journal your feelings and light a candle.



This is a reckoning.



\*\*A hard, honest look at the man you have become, the lies you have been told, and the patterns that are keeping you trapped.\*\* You were taught that strength means silence. That real men don't ask for help. That if you just push harder, everything will work out.



It didn't. And you know it.



The Reckoning is for the man who is tired of the act. Who knows something is broken but doesn't know where to start. This book will show you.



No shortcuts. No excuses. Just the truth and the tools to rebuild.
