Romance is a $1.4 billion industry — and the most consistently profitable genre on Amazon KDP. But most authors approach it with guesswork: picking a random subgenre, choosing tropes they personally like, and hoping readers find them. That is not a strategy. That is a lottery ticket.

**The Romance Author Toolkit** replaces guesswork with a system. Built by an indie author with 100+ books published across 15+ languages, this 45-page guide gives you every tool, template, and strategy you need to write romance that connects with readers and sells consistently.

**What's inside:**

- 🔥 **Complete subgenre breakdown** — understand where the readers and the money are before you write a single word
- 💕 **30 proven tropes + combo formulas** — the specific story patterns readers actively search for, with mix-and-match combinations that create fresh hooks
- 📐 **8-beat story structure** — the romance-specific narrative framework that satisfies reader expectations every time
- 📋 **30-chapter scene planner** — a fill-in template that turns your idea into a complete outline in one sitting
- 👤 **24 character archetypes** — heroes, heroines, and the psychology that makes readers fall in love with them
- ✍️ **Title generator system** — proven title formulas by subgenre that grab attention and signal genre instantly
- 🔑 **100+ Amazon keywords** — ready-to-use keyword sets organized by subgenre for maximum KDP discoverability
- 📝 **Blurb masterclass** — the exact anatomy of a blurb that converts browsers into buyers, with templates for every subgenre
- 📚 **Series strategy + revenue math** — why series outsell standalones 3-to-1, how to plan one, and the real numbers behind reader read-through
- 💰 **Pricing strategy** — what to charge at every stage, from launch to backlist, including permafree and KU math
- 🚀 **Launch checklist** — a step-by-step launch sequence that maximizes first-week visibility and ranking
- 📖 **Back matter optimization** — turn every reader into a repeat buyer with strategic end-of-book content
- ⚡ **Write faster framework** — techniques to increase daily word count without sacrificing quality
- 🌡️ **Heat level guide** — from sweet to dark, how to calibrate spice to your target audience
- 👥 **ARC team blueprint** — how to build, manage, and leverage an advance reader team for launch reviews
- 📊 **Comp author strategy** — identify and position yourself against comparable authors for Amazon's algorithm
- 📱 **BookTok & social media playbook** — platform-specific strategies for reaching romance readers where they actually are
- ❌ **15 mistakes that kill romance careers** — the pitfalls that derail new romance authors, and how to avoid every one

**This is not theory. This is the playbook.**
