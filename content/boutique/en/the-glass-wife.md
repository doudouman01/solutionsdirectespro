## She was the perfect wife. She was also the most dangerous person in the room.

Caroline Blackwell has spent fourteen years building a flawless life inside her husband's East Hampton estate — hosting galas, raising children, smiling for photographs. To the world, she is the Glass Wife: transparent, beautiful, impossible to see through.

But behind the performance, Caroline has been watching. Documenting. Preparing.

When she discovers that Marcus has hidden forty-three million dollars in offshore accounts — and that his father Harrison has been running a twenty-year institutional fraud — Caroline realizes she holds enough evidence to destroy the Blackwell empire. The question isn't whether she'll use it. It's *when*.

## Marriage as Warfare

As husband and wife wage a silent, devastating war of surveillance and counter-surveillance inside the walls of the most beautiful house on Further Lane, a deeper secret surfaces: Harrison Blackwell has other sons. Hidden children, paid for in silence, managed by attorneys, erased from the family record. And they're about to find out who they are.

Told in alternating first-person chapters by Caroline and Marcus, *The Glass Wife* is a razor-sharp domestic thriller about marriage as warfare, wealth as camouflage, and the terrifying moment when the person you underestimated turns out to be the one holding all the cards.

## What Readers Will Find Inside

- A dual-POV domestic thriller set in the elite world of East Hampton
- A wife who has been playing the long game for fourteen years
- Offshore accounts, institutional fraud, and hidden heirs
- A marriage where every smile is strategy and every silence is a weapon
- Book One of *The Blackwell Legacy* trilogy
