You wrote the book. You edited it, designed the cover, formatted the interior, crafted the description. And then you hit publish — and nothing happened. No sales. No reviews. No visibility. The book sat on Amazon like a message in a bottle thrown into the Pacific, sinking silently while four million other bottles floated past.

This is the default outcome for self-published books. Not because the books are bad, but because their authors treated publication as the finish line instead of the starting line. On Amazon, the first seven days after your book goes live are the most consequential period in its entire commercial life. The algorithm watches what happens in that window and decides how much organic visibility to give your book for the months that follow. A strong first week compounds. A weak first week buries.

**Self-Publishing Launch Playbook** is the system that prevents the silent launch. Built from launching 100+ titles across 15+ languages on Amazon KDP, this 18-page guide gives you the exact sequence — every task, every timeline, every decision — from 30 days before publication to 14 days after.

- 📅 **30-day pre-launch timeline** — every task, in order, with deadlines that keep you on track without overwhelming you
- 📧 **ARC team coordination** — how to recruit, distribute, and time your advance reader team for maximum launch-day review impact
- 🚀 **Launch-day execution plan** — the hour-by-hour sequence for your most important day on Amazon
- 📊 **First-week momentum tactics** — what to do in days 2-7 to sustain the velocity that triggers algorithmic visibility
- 💰 **Promotional pricing strategy** — when to use $0.99 launches, Kindle Countdown Deals, and free promotions for maximum impact
- 📱 **Social media launch playbook** — platform-specific posting templates for BookTok, Instagram, Facebook, and email
- 📈 **Post-launch optimization** — the 14-day follow-up sequence that converts launch momentum into sustained sales
- ✅ **Complete launch checklist** — a printable, step-by-step checklist you can use for every book you publish

**Launch once. Launch right. Let the algorithm do the rest.**
