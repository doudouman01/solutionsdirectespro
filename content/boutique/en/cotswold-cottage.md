When three strangers inherit a dilapidated cottage in the Cotswolds, none of them want to be there. A widow running from grief. A businesswoman running from failure. A young mother running from a marriage that is falling apart.

But the cottage has other plans. And so does the village.

Over one unforgettable summer, these three women will discover that sometimes the life you never planned is the one you were always meant to live.

- A warm, uplifting women's fiction novel set in the Cotswolds
- Three compelling heroines with intertwined stories
- Perfect for fans of heartfelt, feel-good fiction

**Some cottages are just buildings. This one is a second chance.**
