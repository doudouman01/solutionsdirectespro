You are halfway through writing book three when you realize you cannot remember whether the protagonist's sister was mentioned as older or younger in book one. You spend forty-five minutes searching through 180,000 words of previous manuscripts. You find two contradictory references. Your readers — who remember everything — will find them too.

This is what happens without a series bible. And it gets exponentially worse with every book you add.

**Author Series Bible Template** is the system that prevents continuity disasters. Built by an author managing multiple interconnected series across 15+ languages, this 30-page template gives you a structured, fillable framework for tracking every element that matters across a multi-book narrative.

- 👤 **Character profiles** — physical descriptions, personality traits, relationships, motivations, wounds, arcs, and book-by-book development tracking for every major and recurring character
- 🗺️ **World-building reference** — locations, geography, political structures, cultural norms, technology levels, magic systems, and rules — organized for instant reference during drafting
- 📅 **Timeline tracker** — chronological event mapping across books, with parallel timeline support for multiple POV characters and subplot threading
- 📖 **Plot arc mapping** — series-level overarching arcs, book-level arcs, and subplot arcs tracked visually so you can see how every story thread connects
- 🔗 **Continuity checklist** — the specific details that trip up series authors (physical descriptions, place names, relationship statuses, revealed secrets, power levels) with a system for verifying consistency before each new book
- 📋 **Release scheduling** — publication timeline planner for coordinating writing, editing, cover design, and launch dates across multiple books in a series
- 🧩 **Cliffhanger and setup tracker** — what you planted in previous books that must pay off in future ones, so no reader expectation is left unfulfilled
- 📚 **Series architecture models** — structural templates for 3-book trilogies, 5-book arcs, 7+ book series, companion standalones, and shared-universe formats

**Your readers remember everything. Your series bible makes sure you do too.**
