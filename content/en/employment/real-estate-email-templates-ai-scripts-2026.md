# Real Estate Email Templates: 10 AI-Powered Scripts That Convert in 2026

The average real estate agent spends 5-7 hours per week writing emails. Lead follow-ups, showing confirmations, market updates, review requests, referral asks — each one drafted from scratch, each one eating into time that could be spent with clients.

Here's the smarter approach: **10 proven email templates, each paired with an AI prompt to customize it in under 2 minutes.** Copy the template, run the prompt, personalize one or two details, hit send.

These aren't generic "Dear Valued Client" templates. They're built for conversion — tested against actual response rates and refined for the way people communicate in 2026.

## How to Use These Templates

Each template below includes:
- **The script** — ready to copy and customize
- **When to send it** — timing matters as much as content
- **The AI prompt** — paste this into ChatGPT or Claude to generate a personalized version

**The golden rule:** Always add one personal detail. A generic template gets ignored. A template with one sentence that proves you remember the recipient gets opened, read, and answered.

## Template 1: New Lead Response (Speed to Lead)

**When to send:** Within 5 minutes of receiving the lead. Research shows responding within 5 minutes makes you 21 times more likely to qualify the lead.

**The script:**

> Hi [First Name],
>
> Thanks for reaching out about [property/area they inquired about]. I'd love to help you find the right fit.
>
> Quick question to get us started: are you looking to move within the next 1-3 months, or are you still in the early research phase? Either way, I can point you toward some options you won't find just browsing online.
>
> I'm available [today/tomorrow] for a quick 10-minute call if that's helpful — no pressure, just a conversation.
>
> [Your Name]
> [Phone] | [Brokerage]

**AI prompt to customize:**

```
You are a friendly, professional real estate agent in [YOUR CITY].
Write a first-response email to a new lead named [NAME] who 
inquired about [PROPERTY/AREA/PRICE RANGE] through [SOURCE — 
Zillow/website/social media]. Keep it under 80 words. Include 
one specific detail about the area or property type they asked 
about. End with a soft CTA — a question, not a demand. Tone: 
warm, knowledgeable, zero pressure.
```

## Template 2: Open House Follow-Up

**When to send:** Within 24 hours of the open house.

**The script:**

> Hi [First Name],
>
> Great meeting you at the open house on [Street Name] yesterday. I noticed you spent extra time looking at [specific area — the kitchen, the backyard, the primary suite] — it really is [one genuine positive about that feature].
>
> Since you mentioned [something they said — school district, commute, timeline], I pulled together a couple of comparable listings in the area that might interest you. Happy to share those whenever you're ready.
>
> Any questions about the property? I'm here.
>
> [Your Name]

**AI prompt to customize:**

```
Write a follow-up email to [NAME] who attended my open house at 
[ADDRESS] yesterday. They showed particular interest in [FEATURE].
They mentioned [CONCERN OR PRIORITY]. Reference both naturally. 
Offer to share comparable listings as added value. Under 100 
words. Personal, not salesy. Sign off as [YOUR NAME], [BROKERAGE].
```

## Template 3: Cold Lead Re-Engagement (60+ Days Silent)

**When to send:** When a lead hasn't responded in 60+ days. Send a 3-email sequence, spaced 5 days apart.

**Email 1 — The Value Drop:**

> Hi [First Name],
>
> I know life gets busy, so I won't take much of your time. I noticed [a relevant market change — new listings in their target area, a price drop on a property they viewed, a market shift].
>
> Thought of you and wanted to pass it along — no strings attached.
>
> If your plans have changed, no worries at all. If you're still thinking about [buying/selling], I'm here whenever the timing is right.
>
> [Your Name]

**Email 2 — The Resource:**

> Hi [First Name],
>
> I put together a quick guide on [relevant topic — "5 things to know before buying in [area]" / "what sellers in [area] are doing differently this fall"]. Figured it might be useful whether you're moving next month or next year.
>
> [Link or attachment]
>
> Let me know if anything catches your eye.
>
> [Your Name]

**Email 3 — The Honest Close:**

> Hi [First Name],
>
> I want to respect your inbox, so this is my last check-in for now. If the timing isn't right, I completely understand — plans change, and that's perfectly fine.
>
> If anything shifts down the road, you know where to find me. Wishing you all the best either way.
>
> [Your Name]

**AI prompt for the full sequence:**

```
Create a 3-email re-engagement sequence for a buyer lead named 
[NAME] who went silent 60+ days ago. They were interested in 
[PROPERTY TYPE] in [AREA] with a budget around [AMOUNT]. Space 
emails 5 days apart. Email 1: share a relevant market insight. 
Email 2: offer a useful resource. Email 3: honest, respectful 
close. Each under 80 words. Never use "just checking in" or 
"circling back." Tone: patient expert, not hungry salesperson.
```

## Template 4: Weekly Seller Update

**When to send:** Every Friday for active listings.

**The script:**

> Hi [First Name],
>
> Here's your weekly update on [Property Address]:
>
> **This week:** [X] showings | [X] online views | [X] saves on [platform]
>
> **Feedback highlights:** [1-2 sentence summary of showing feedback]
>
> **Market context:** [One relevant data point — new comparable sale, inventory change, days-on-market trend]
>
> **Next steps:** This coming week, I'm [specific action — adjusting the photo lineup, boosting the social media campaign, reaching out to agents who showed interest].
>
> Questions or concerns? I'm always just a call or text away.
>
> [Your Name]

**AI prompt:**

```
Write a weekly seller update email for my listing at [ADDRESS]. 
This week: [X] showings, [X] views on Zillow, [feedback summary].
Include one market data point for context and one specific action 
I'm taking next week. Under 120 words. Transparent, proactive, 
reassuring. Sign off as [YOUR NAME].
```

## Template 5: Buyer Consultation Invitation

**When to send:** After initial qualification, when you want to schedule a deeper conversation.

**The script:**

> Hi [First Name],
>
> Based on our conversation, it sounds like you have a clear picture of what you're looking for — and I think I can help narrow the search significantly.
>
> I'd love to set up a 30-minute buyer consultation where we can:
> - Map out your must-haves vs. nice-to-haves
> - Review the current inventory in [target area]
> - Discuss strategy (there are a few things happening in the market right now that could work in your favor)
>
> Would [Day] at [Time] work, or is there a better time for you?
>
> [Your Name]

**AI prompt:**

```
Write a buyer consultation invitation email to [NAME], a 
[first-time buyer / move-up buyer / investor] interested in 
[AREA]. Reference something specific from our previous 
conversation: [DETAIL]. Frame the consultation as valuable for 
them, not as a sales meeting. Include 3 bullet points of what 
we'll cover. Offer two specific time options. Under 100 words.
```

## Template 6: Google Review Request

**When to send:** 1-2 weeks after closing, once the initial excitement has settled.

**The script:**

> Hi [First Name],
>
> I hope you're settling into [the new place / life after the sale] beautifully. It was genuinely a pleasure working with you — especially [one specific memorable moment from the transaction].
>
> If you have 2 minutes, a Google review would mean the world to me. It helps other [buyers/sellers] find an agent they can trust — and your experience is the best endorsement I could ask for.
>
> [Direct Google Review Link]
>
> Thank you — and don't hesitate to reach out if anything comes up with the [home/move].
>
> [Your Name]

**AI prompt:**

```
Write a Google review request to [CLIENT NAME] who just 
[bought/sold] a [PROPERTY TYPE] in [AREA] [TIMEFRAME ago]. 
Reference one specific positive moment from our transaction: 
[MOMENT]. Keep it genuine and brief — under 60 words. Include 
a placeholder [REVIEW LINK]. Not pushy — one ask, sincere 
gratitude.
```

## Template 7: Referral Request

**When to send:** 30 days after closing, or during a check-in with a past client.

**The script:**

> Hi [First Name],
>
> I hope [the new home is treating you well / things have settled nicely since the sale]. I think about our [transaction story — "search through half the listings in Oakwood" / "negotiation marathon"] and smile.
>
> I have a small ask: if anyone in your circle mentions they're thinking about buying or selling, I'd be honored if you thought of me. The best clients I've ever worked with have come through referrals from people like you — and I promise to take just as good care of them.
>
> No pressure at all — just planting the seed. And if YOU ever need anything real estate-related, I'm always a text away.
>
> [Your Name]

## Template 8: Monthly Market Update Newsletter

**When to send:** First week of each month.

**The script:**

> **[Month] Market Update: [Area]**
>
> Hi [First Name],
>
> Here's what happened in [Area] real estate this month:
>
> 📊 **Median sale price:** $[X] ([up/down X%] from last month)
> 🏠 **Active listings:** [X] ([more/fewer] than last month)
> ⏱️ **Average days on market:** [X] days
>
> **What this means for buyers:** [One actionable sentence]
> **What this means for sellers:** [One actionable sentence]
>
> **My take:** [One sentence of genuine local insight — not a sales pitch]
>
> Questions about how this affects your plans? Reply to this email — I read every one.
>
> [Your Name]

**AI prompt:**

```
Write a 200-word monthly market update email for [CITY/AREA] for
[MONTH YEAR]. Data: median price $[X] (change [Y]%), active 
listings [X], average DOM [X] days. Include one insight each for 
buyers and sellers, plus one sentence of personal market 
commentary. Tone: knowledgeable local expert having coffee with 
a friend. No jargon.
```

## Template 10: Closing Day Thank You

**When to send:** Day of closing or the morning after.

**The script:**

> Hi [First Name(s)],
>
> Congratulations — it's officially yours. 🎉
>
> Working with you over the past [timeframe] has been a genuine highlight of my year. I'll always remember [one specific moment — "when we walked into the kitchen and you both just looked at each other and knew" / "your determination to find exactly the right school district"].
>
> I hope [the new address] brings you [relevant wish — "years of family dinners around that incredible island" / "the fresh start you were looking for"].
>
> If anything comes up — a contractor recommendation, a question about the neighborhood, or just someone to celebrate with — you know where to find me.
>
> With gratitude,
> [Your Name]

**AI prompt:**

```
Write a heartfelt closing day message to [CLIENT NAME(S)] who 
just [bought/sold] a [PROPERTY TYPE] in [AREA]. Reference one 
specific memorable moment: [MOMENT]. Include a warm wish for 
their future. Add a subtle, natural referral mention — not a 
formal ask, just "if anyone you know is thinking about a move, 
I'd love to help them the way I got to help you." Under 100 
words. Genuine, not templated.
```
