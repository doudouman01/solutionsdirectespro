Most financial advice fails because it treats money as a math problem. It's not. **It's an emotional problem.**

You don't need another budget app. You need to understand why you sabotage yourself financially, why you spend when you're stressed, and why wealth feels like something that happens to other people.

This book takes you through a proven framework to identify your inherited money beliefs, dismantle the ones that don't serve you, and build a new financial identity from the ground up.

**The goal isn't just to have more money. It's to stop being afraid of it.**
