In a near-future world where technology has woven itself into every aspect of daily life, a mysterious frequency begins to emerge — undetectable by conventional instruments, yet devastating in its effects. People are changing. Minds are shifting. And no one can explain why.

When the first anomalies surface, they're dismissed as glitches — isolated incidents in an increasingly connected world. But as the pattern grows, one person begins to see the terrifying truth behind the static: this isn't a malfunction. It's a weapon.

**Dead Frequency** is a pulse-pounding near-future sci-fi thriller that explores the dark intersection of technology, consciousness, and control. In a world where the line between human and machine grows thinner by the day, the most dangerous signal isn't the one you can hear — it's the one you can't.

*"The frequency is dead. But what it left behind is very much alive."*
