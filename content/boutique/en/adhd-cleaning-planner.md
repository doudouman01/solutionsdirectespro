"Clean the house" equals paralysis. "Do one 10-minute sprint" equals doable. The *ADHD Cleaning Planner* is a 35-page printable system that turns overwhelming chores into short, rewarding bursts your brain can actually get behind.

Built around a simple 4-step sprint method — pick a room, set a timer, sprint for 10 minutes, reward yourself — this planner eliminates the decision fatigue that keeps you frozen on the couch. A random room picker removes the "where do I even start" problem, and 10 room-specific checklists (kitchen, bathroom, bedroom, living room, office, laundry, kids' room, entryway, car and garage) tell you exactly what to do when you get there.

Quick win cards give you tasks that take under 2 minutes for instant momentum. The emergency guest sprint protocol saves you when someone texts "I'm 20 minutes away." A cleaning habit stacker, reward tracker and 31-day monthly tracker help you build consistency without perfection.

Print it out or load it on your tablet — the clean PDF format works beautifully with GoodNotes, Notability or plain old paper and pen.
