# Funeral Bookmark Template — Religious Cross Design

A memorial bookmark is the keepsake guests are most likely to use every day. Tucked inside a Bible, a journal or a favorite book, it becomes a quiet, recurring reminder of someone loved. This template features a navy, gold and cream cross design with space for the loved one's name, dates and a meaningful verse or quote.

The 2.5 x 7 inch format is the standard bookmark size — slim enough to fit in any book, substantial enough to carry real meaning. Edit the text in Microsoft Word, print on cardstock, and trim to size. The design includes crop marks to make cutting precise and effortless, even without professional tools.

Instant digital download. You receive the editable Word (.docx) file and a PDF instruction guide immediately after purchase. Print as many bookmarks as you need — hand them out during the service or mail them to family members who could not attend.
