When Evie inherits a failing bookshop in a sleepy Devon seaside town, she sees it as an escape from a life that fell apart. No plan. No money. No idea what she is doing.

But Seabrook has a way of pulling people in. The locals. The cliffs. The smell of salt and old pages. And a mysterious box of manuscripts hidden in the back room that someone went to great lengths to keep secret.

- A charming, heartfelt women's fiction novel set in coastal Devon
- A bookshop full of secrets and second chances
- Perfect for fans of cosy, character-driven stories

**Some bookshops sell stories. This one holds them.**
