Your family is drowning in screens. You know it. Your kids know it. And every night, the same hollow dinner plays out — four people, four devices, zero conversation.

This book changes that. Not with guilt. Not with scare tactics. With a proven, step-by-step system that starts with you and transforms your entire household.

**Here's what makes this different:** Most screen-time books lecture parents about their children. This one asks you to go first. Before you set a single rule for your kids, you'll complete your own 72-hour digital reset — so when the hard conversations come, you speak from experience, not authority.

Then you'll learn what screens actually do to your child's developing brain, why your guilt is the algorithm's greatest ally, and how to build a family culture where intentional technology use is simply who you are — not a set of rules you enforce.

**Based on current neuroscience and behavioral research**, this book gives you the understanding, the tools, and the confidence to reclaim your family's attention, connection, and presence — without banning technology or becoming the enemy.

**Book 2 in the Digital Wellness Series** by Adrian Phoenix Vale. Works as a standalone guide or as the natural next step after *The 14-Day Dopamine Reset*.

The family that sits around the table tonight — present, connected, choosing each other over the infinite scroll — is the family you can build. It starts with turning the page.
