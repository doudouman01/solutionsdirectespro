## Plan Before You Shop

The biggest source of grocery overspending is impulse buying. A simple meal plan for the week can cut your bill by 20-30%.

## Buy Seasonal Produce

Fruits and vegetables in season are cheaper, fresher, and taste better. Check what's in season in your area and plan meals around those ingredients.

## Compare Unit Prices

Don't just look at the sticker price. Compare the price per kilogram or per litre. The larger package isn't always the better deal.

## Use a Shopping List — and Stick to It

Write your list based on your meal plan. Once you're in the store, stick to it. Avoid browsing aisles you don't need.

## Consider Store Brands

Store brands often use the same suppliers as name brands. Try them — you might not notice the difference, but your wallet will.

## Batch Cook and Freeze

Cooking in larger batches and freezing portions saves both time and money. It also reduces food waste.

> The goal isn't to spend as little as possible — it's to spend wisely on food that nourishes you.
