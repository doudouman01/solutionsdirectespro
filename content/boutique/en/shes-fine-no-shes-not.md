You've been holding it together for so long that you've forgotten what it feels like to fall apart. And that's the problem.

Inside, you'll find practical exercises, real-world strategies, and honest conversations about the patterns that keep women stuck in cycles of self-sacrifice, guilt, and emotional exhaustion.

No fluff. No toxic positivity. Just the truth you've been avoiding and the roadmap you've been looking for.
