She wakes up in a hospital room with no memory of how she got there.

The doctors say she had an accident. Her husband says she fell down the stairs. Her best friend says she jumped.

**Three versions of the truth. None of them are hers.**

As fragments of memory begin to surface, she realizes that the life she's been living might not be the one she chose. Someone has been making decisions for her. Someone has been watching.

And the closer she gets to the truth, the more dangerous it becomes to remember.

- A psychological thriller that plays with perception and reality
- An unreliable narrator you'll love and doubt in equal measure
- A twist ending that reframes everything you thought you knew
- Perfect for fans of dark, atmospheric suspense

**Some doors, once opened, can never be closed again.**
