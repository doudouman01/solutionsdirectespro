When Maren inherits a decaying manor deep in the Victorian countryside, she expects cobwebs and paperwork. What she finds is something far worse.

Locked rooms. Cold spots. Whispers in the walls. And a portrait of a woman who looks exactly like her.

The locals say Thornwood is cursed. The estate agent says it is a bargain. And the man who lives in the cottage at the edge of the property says nothing at all.

- An Australian gothic romance dripping with atmosphere
- A heroine caught between the living and the dead
- A crumbling manor with secrets buried in every wall

**Some inheritances come with strings. This one comes with chains.**
